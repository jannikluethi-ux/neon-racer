Surge LMP car model - credits
=============================

This work is based on "Concept Sport Car" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/concept-sport-car-566075bdb499404b908895a5f4dc6aa0) by KTKR (https://sketchfab.com/KTKR) licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

Modifications (made for Neon Racer, Nov 2026):
- Branding removed: the wheel centre caps carried a logo (lettering islands on
  the hub). Hubs and caps were deleted and replaced with plain procedural hubs
  and gold centre caps. No other badges or marks were found on the body; the
  blank rear licence plate became a race-number plate.
- New PBR materials built from scratch: metallic gold paint with clear coat,
  satin black / carbon trim (hood vent panel, splitter, diffuser, arches),
  gloss-black roof (was copper), gunmetal intake surrounds and pillars (were
  copper / silver), dark tinted glass, emissive white LED headlights / DRLs and
  red tail lights, matte tyres, satin-black rims.
- Race number #51 added as large plates on both doors (white numerals on a
  black plate with a thin gold border) and on the rear plate.
- Brake discs and gold brake calipers were added (the source wheels had none).
  The small bolts on the rim lip were removed because they became spikes when
  the wheels were reduced.
- A zero-volume helper strip at the cowl and degenerate faces were removed.
  The wheels were split into four named wheel nodes. Each wheel's origin is at
  its centre. The front tyres were lowered 1 cm so that all four touch the
  ground.
- Geometry was reduced with meshoptimizer to three levels of detail (about
  60k / 26k / 8k triangles). Materials were merged to 12 draw calls per LOD
  using tiny palette textures. Shading was smoothed by angle and uses weighted
  normals; no triangles were added. Meshopt-compressed variants are included.
- Re-centred and scaled uniformly: metres, ground at y=0, the midpoint between
  the axles at the origin, wheelbase 2.82 m (the game's Surge), length 4.85 m.
