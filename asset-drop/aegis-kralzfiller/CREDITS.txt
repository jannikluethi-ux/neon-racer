Aegis #88 car model - credits
=============================

This work is based on "2026 Kralzfiller Trackday V10" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/2026-kralzfiller-trackday-v10-d486b2662d314db8b5afc71bd1488c3c) by Dave Love SketchFab (https://sketchfab.com/Tyler_Dave) licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

Modifications (made for Neon Racer, Nov 2026):
- Branding and text removed: the donut-textured nose badge, the glowing
  "TRACKDAY V10" wing text and a second "Text" mesh on the wing, two
  dashboard display plates carrying another game's material name, and a
  mechanical texture sheet with real manufacturer logos (all source
  textures were dropped; every surface now uses a plain colour atlas).
- Livery repainted as a deep electric blue with gloss-black carbon aero,
  neon-cyan brake calipers and gunmetal rims. Race number #88 added as
  black plates with white numerals and a cyan keyline on both cockpit-pod
  sides and on the nose.
- Removed: wiper arms / rod across the canopy, a duplicate fully transparent
  canopy shell, two small sidepod vanes that covered the number area, and
  two stray micro-quads.
- Canopy glass re-made as a single dark-tinted shell (alpha 0.90, no
  transmission). All materials double-sided.
- Wheels split into Wheel_FL/FR/RL/RR (tyre, rim, cap, centre nut, barrel,
  disc) and Caliper_FL/FR/RL/RR (caliper + upright), origins at the wheel
  centres. Wheel diameters, wheelbase and track are the source proportions,
  uniformly scaled.
- Geometry reduced with meshoptimizer (gltfpack) to three levels of detail
  (about 61k / 24k / 8k triangles), merged to 4 materials / 11 draw calls
  per LOD. Meshopt + KTX2 (UASTC) variants included.
- Re-centred and scaled: metres, ground at y=0, midpoint between the axles at
  the origin, length 4.50 m.

The modified model is shared under the same CC-BY-4.0 terms. Keep the credit
line above in the game's credits / about screen and in the repository.
