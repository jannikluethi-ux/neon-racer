# Vehicle models

These files are the visual bodies for three cars. Surge stays a procedural model in the game and has no file here.

| Car | Asset | Files | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vector | Race Car 01 | `lod0/vector-gt3.glb`, `lod1/vector-gt3.glb`, `lod2/vector-gt3.glb` | https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/race-car-01-7243b4952d4341ff96d2c5a9ff431238 | [CC-BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| Vanta | Selva Hypercar concept by Max Hordin 2019 | `lod0/vanta-gt4.glb`, `lod1/vanta-gt4.glb`, `lod2/vanta-gt4.glb` | https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/selva-hypercar-concept-by-max-hordin-2019-e426a6e57b364b3fa6c29775c72cac05 | [CC-BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| Aegis | Family Sedan | `lod1/family-sedan.glb`, `lod2/family-sedan.glb` | https://tevy.dev/models/family-sedan | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |

Aegis — author tevy. Commercial use, modification, and redistribution are allowed. Attribution is not required. The GLB is the publisher's LOD1 and LOD2 build (meshopt geometry, KTX2 textures).

Vector — this work is based on "Race Car 01" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/race-car-01-7243b4952d4341ff96d2c5a9ff431238) by toddeppe (https://sketchfab.com/toddeppe) licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Modifications made for Neon Racer, November 2026: Goodyear, Le Mans, sponsor and "01" markings painted out; livery moved to cyan and black; interior reduced; three levels of detail packed as meshopt + KTX2; wheels split into named nodes. The game mounts that body on the existing Vector wheel rig and draws #27 on the blank number panels. The modified model stays under CC-BY-4.0. The same credit line is on the profile screen.

Vanta — this work is based on "Selva Hypercar concept by Max Hordin 2019" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/selva-hypercar-concept-by-max-hordin-2019-e426a6e57b364b3fa6c29775c72cac05) by Max Hordin (https://sketchfab.com/maxhordin) licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Modifications made for Neon Racer, November 2026: manufacturer marks and the license plate removed; livery repainted deep red with #04 plates on the doors, rear and hood; a carbon GT4 wing added; wheels split into named nodes and given procedural rims; three levels of detail packed as meshopt. The game mounts that body on the existing Vanta wheel rig. The modified model stays under CC-BY-4.0. The same credit line is on the profile screen.

Physics, wheel positions, and collision are not taken from any of these files. Race splitters, sills, and the wing on Aegis are original parts modelled in the game.
